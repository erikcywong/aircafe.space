What Is Already True, and What Is Not

哪些已经是事实，哪些还不是

These pages have one job: to stop anyone mistaking a validated pilot for a running business. The left column is measured. The right column is empty, and it will stay listed as empty until it is not.

这几页只有一个任务：不让任何人把一个已验证的中试误认为一门正在运转的生意。左边一栏是实测数据，右边一栏是空的——并且在它不再为空之前，它会一直以空的状态被列着。

3.2

What Has Not Been Done尚未完成的部分

Not done — and we will not imply otherwise未完成——我们不会暗示相反

  • No mass production. The commercial module exists as an engineered design, not as steel on the ground.未量产。商业级模块目前是工程图纸，不是已落地运转的设备。
  • No scaled revenue. Revenue to date is negligible; there is no commercial shipment record.无规模化收入。累计收入可忽略，无商业出货记录。
  • No audited financial statements.无审计财务报表。
  • No purchase orders or letters of intent from plantation customers.未取得种植园客户的采购订单或意向书。
  • Patent is not yet granted — preliminary examination only.专利尚未授权——仅完成初步审查。
  • No third-party cupping panel has scored the output.尚未由第三方杯测机构对产出评分。
  • No ESG, water-footprint or carbon baseline certified by an accredited body.尚无权威机构认证的 ESG、水足迹或碳基线。

What we do have我们已经有的

  • A filed invention patent on the full-cycle method and system, preliminary examination passed 2026-09-09.一项已提交的发明专利，覆盖全周期方法与系统，2026-09-09 初审合格。
  • A working pilot process that repeatedly produces transplant-grade seedlings in 4 months.一套可运转的中试工艺，可重复地在 4 个月内产出达移栽标准的幼苗。
  • A multi-batch parameter library — the compounding asset described in the technology section.一份多批次参数库——即技术章节所述的复利型资产。
  • A complete cost model with line-itemed capex and opex per standard module.一套完整成本模型，含单标准模块的资本开支与运营成本明细。
  • Two co-founders with the operating record to have produced the prototype, the cupping result, the sensor layer and the patent filing — one on hardware and systems, one on operations and commercial scale — with three senior seats scoped and funded under this round. See Founding Team.两位有完整操作记录的联合创始人——原型机、杯测结果、传感层与专利申报均出自二人之手，一位负责硬件与系统，一位负责营运与商业规模化；本轮已界定并预算三个高级岗位。详见创始团队。
  • A climate-driven demand thesis corroborated by ICO research, not by our own extrapolation.一条气候驱动的需求逻辑，由 ICO 研究支撑，而非我方外推。
