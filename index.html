AIRCAFE has validated the core process of aeroponic coffee seedling production: a 4-month growth cycle, 3 batches per year, ¥6.61 cost per seedling against a ¥25 selling price — a 73.5% unit gross margin. 02

The Three Things That Matter三件最重要的事

🧬 What we actually built

🧬 我们真正做出来的东西

Not a container farm, not a piece of hardware. A gas-liquid aeroponic full-cycle management method and system — a closed loop that reads root-zone conditions, adjusts nutrient mist, timing and light, and returns the data to the recipe. Coffee seedlings reach transplant grade in 4 months, three times a year, with no soil and no pesticides.

不是集装箱农场，也不是单台设备，而是一套《基于气液式雾化的全周期种植管理方法及系统》——读取根系微环境、动态调节营养雾配比与喷淋时序、光照，并将数据回流至配方模型的闭环系统。咖啡幼苗 4 个月达到移栽标准，一年三批，不用土、不打药。

📐 We sell seedlings, not coffee

📐 我们卖苗，不卖豆

This is the single most important framing. The customer is the coffee plantation, the nursery operator, the replanting programme — not the roaster, not the supply-chain procurement desk. No brand war, no retail shelf, no price fight with incumbents. A ¥6.61 cost against a ¥25 market price, replicated module by module.

这是最关键的一点。客户是咖啡种植园、苗木商、复种计划——不是烘焙商，不是供应链采购部门。没有品牌战、没有货架之争、不与现有玩家打价格战。¥6.61 成本对 ¥25 市场价，模块化逐台复制。

🎯 Why now, and why a trial field

🎯 为什么是现在，为什么是这块试验田

We are inside the window where the patent has passed preliminary examination but has not yet been granted, and the process is validated but not yet industrialised. AIRCAFE's stated thesis — invest early, invest small, dual-carbon technology, rural and industrial internet, AI + hardware — maps onto this asset almost line for line, with Xiaomi-ecosystem supply-chain capability as the multiplier.

我们正处在一个特定窗口：专利已初审合格但尚未授权，工艺已验证但尚未工业化。AIRCAFE公开的投资主张——投早、投小、双碳科技、农村互联网与产业互联网、AI+硬件——与本项目几乎逐条对应，而小米生态的硬件供应链能力则是放大器。

We are not asking AIRCAFE to buy seedlings. We are inviting AIRCAFE to be the first institutional capital in a patented control system that turns coffee cultivation into a manufactured process.

我们不打算把幼苗卖给你。我们邀请的是：成为这套受专利保护的控制系统的第一位机构资本——这套系统把咖啡种植变成一道可被制造的工艺。
