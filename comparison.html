2.4

Why "Method + System" Is the Right Moat为什么「方法+系统」才是对的护城河

A device can be copied. A method is harder.设备可以被抄，方法更难。

Anyone can buy a nozzle and mount it in a box. What cannot be reverse-engineered from a photograph is the schedule that governs when that nozzle fires, for how long, at what pressure, in which sequence across a four-month cycle. We claimed the schedule, not the hardware.

任何人都能买一个喷嘴装进箱子里。无法从一张照片逆向出来的，是那套决定喷嘴何时启动、持续多久、以多大压力、在四个月周期中按什么顺序执行的排程。我们主张的是排程，不是硬件。

Method + system covers both the what and the how方法+系统同时覆盖「做什么」和「怎么做」

A method claim alone leaves a competitor free to build a different apparatus that achieves the same result. A system claim alone leaves them free to run a different process on the same apparatus. Claiming both closes the obvious work-arounds.

只主张方法，竞争者可以换一台设备达成同样结果；只主张系统，竞争者可以在同一设备上跑另一套工艺。两者同时主张，才能堵住显而易见的绕行路径。

The parameter pack is the compounding half参数包是复利的那一半

The patent is a legal fence. The parameter library is an operational one: each batch makes the next cheaper and more consistent, and that library is deliberately kept out of the published application. Two layers, two different failure modes covered.

专利是一道法律围栏，参数库是一道运营围栏：每个批次都让下一批更便宜、更一致，而这份库有意排除于公开申请文件之外。两层结构，覆盖两种不同的失效模式。

What this is not它不是什么

It is not a granted patent, and it is not a guarantee of exclusivity. Preliminary examination is a formal gate, not a novelty finding. The claim may narrow, and a determined competitor with capital may still design around it.

它不是一项已授权专利，也不构成独占保证。初步审查是形式门槛，不是新颖性认定。权利要求可能被限缩，资金充足的竞争者仍可能绕开设计。
