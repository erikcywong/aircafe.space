7.4 · Supply Diagnosis7.4 · 供应链诊断 Stage strategy阶段策略 BeanStar · 比星咖啡 2026-10-09

Technology insurance and joint validation — the strategy for the seedling stage.

科技保险与联合验证 —— 幼苗期的实战策略。

We claim no purchase commitment. The ask is one store scene and your cupping system; the give is priority supply and investment rights, a zero-cost technology narrative, and a line of attack confined to the premium and limited products.

我们不索取采购承诺。索取的是一个门店场景和你们的杯测体系；给出的是优先供应权与优先投资权、零成本的技术叙事，以及一条只打高端线与限定款的进攻路线。

7.4

The working strategy at the seedling stage三 · 目前阶段（幼苗期）的实战策略

Since we cannot offer volume yet, we offer certainty, validation, and a technical anchor your next financing can stand on.

既然量给不了，我们就给确定性、给验证、给一个可以写进融资故事的技术支点。

The technology-insurance capacity ('climate insurance') and joint-validation partner of BeanStar's specialty supply chain.比星精品供应链的 科技保险（Climate Insurance Capacity） 与 联合验证伙伴。

Outward-facing phrasing: 'Your specialty line is hostage to the traditional supply chain. We are your climate-insurance capacity — when traditional beans spike or run short, we step in immediately, with an identical flavour profile.'对外的说法可以是这样一句话：「比星的精品线现在受制于传统供应链。我们要做你们的『气候保险产能』—— 当传统豆涨价或断供时，我这边可以立刻补位，且风味完全一致。」

A joint validation, not a purchase contract是「联合验证」，不是「采购合同」

We are still at the seedling stage, but the patent has passed preliminary examination. We want BeanStar's stores to be the first industrial validation scene. At this stage we claim no purchase commitment and ask for four things, all low-risk for both sides.我们还在幼苗期，但专利已初审合格。我们想用比星的门店作为首个产业验证场景。这个阶段我们不索取采购承诺，只索取四件对双方都低风险的东西。

What we ask BeanStar to put in我们要比星投入什么

  • Expert feedback — structured comments on each sample batch from your sourcing / R&D / QC teams.专家反馈 —— 采购／研发／品控团队对各批次样品的结构化意见。
  • Cupping support — letting our samples enter your existing sensory evaluation process.杯测支持 —— 允许我们的样品进入贵方既有的感官评估流程。
  • One real usage scene — e.g. a limited-edition or experience-store tasting, to collect end-customer feedback.一个真实的使用场景 —— 例如限定款或体验店的试饮，用于收集终端消费者反馈。
  • No more than one review meeting — within management's existing time budget.不超过一次的复盘会议 —— 不占用管理层的时间预算。

What BeanStar gets比星能拿到什么

  • Priority supply rights after validation — locking the first schedulable, stable flavour-base lots as capacity ramps.验证成功后的优先供应权 —— 在产能爬坡阶段锁定第一批可排产的稳定风味基豆。
  • Priority investment rights — entering early rounds as the technology-validation party.优先投资权 —— 在早期轮次以技术验证方的身份进入。
  • A zero-cost technology narrative — 'zero-carbon / tech-cultivation' labels ready for brand communication and ESG disclosure.零成本的技术叙事 —— 「零碳／科技种植」标签可直接用于品牌传播与 ESG 披露。
  • Comparison data with no obligations attached — even if we do not end up working together, you keep a cost-and-flavour benchmark you did not have.一份不含义务的对照数据 —— 即使最终不合作，贵方也拿到了一份既有的成本与风味对照。

Attack the premium and limited lines; leave the mass line alone主攻高端线和限定款，不碰大众线

This is discipline, and it is also good manners. The cost floor of the mass line is commercial beans — aeroponics cannot get there yet. But the premium line (¥30+ limited editions) is different: traditional Geisha is extremely expensive and unstable, while an 'engineered specialty flavour-base bean' is a cost that this price band can absorb.这是纪律，也是分寸。大众线的成本极限是商业豆，雾化水培目前还打不下来。但高端线（例如 30 元以上的限定款）完全不同 —— 传统瑰夏成本极高、供给极不稳定，而用工程化手段生产出来的「精品风味基豆」，在这个价格带里成本是可以被消化的。

The narrative anchor: patent + food-tech叙事支点：「专利 + 食品科技」

Franchisees hear the single-store model; investors hear the valuation logic. In the window of the 1,000-store target and the next financing round, BeanStar needs a new technical anchor — and the keywords of that anchor are exactly the ones we are strong at:对加盟商讲的是单店模型，对投资人讲的是估值逻辑。比星在千店目标与下一轮融资的窗口期，需要一个新的技术支点，而这个支点的关键词正好是我们擅长的那几个：

Engineerable可工程化

Farming → manufacturing: capacity that can be scheduled, costs that can be budgeted, quality that can be parameterised.农业 → 工业：产能可排产、成本可预算、品质可参数化。

IP moatIP 壁垒

The gas-liquid mist full-cycle patent — licensable, and exclusivity is on the table.《基于气液式雾化的全周期种植管理方法及系统》专利，可授权、可独占。

ESGESG

Zero-carbon, pesticide-free, water-saving, local short chain — ready for disclosure documents.零碳、无农药、节水、本地短链 —— 可直接进披露文件。

Food-tech supply chain食品科技供应链

Re-rating a coffee supply chain from 'agriculture' to 'food technology'.把咖啡供应链从「农业估值」重估为「食品科技估值」。

'You sell specialty beans in a ¥15–20 band and plan to reach 1,000 stores within two years. In the traditional supply chain that has no solution, because specialty beans have a yield ceiling.
We turn it into an engineering problem: no terroir, no weather constraint, programmable flavour. Our volume is still small, so we are not asking for orders — we are asking for one store scene and your cupping system, to validate our beans.
If it validates, you gain a backup supply chain that hedges climate and price risk. If it does not, you have lost a few hours.'「你们要在 15–20 元的价格带里卖精品豆，还要在两年内开到 1,000 家店 —— 这件事在传统供应链里是没有解的，因为精品豆的产量有天花板。
我们做的是把这件事变成工程问题：不靠风土、不受气候约束、风味可编程。我们现在量还小，所以不求订单 —— 只求你们给我们一个门店场景，用你们的杯测体系验证我们的豆子。
验证通过，你们拿到一条能对冲气候与价格风险的备用供应链；验证不通过，你们损失几个小时。」
