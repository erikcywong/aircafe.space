The Trigger Is a Land Problem, Not a Price Problem

触发条件是土地问题，不是价格问题

Coffee has had bad price years before and recovered. What is different this time is that the growing area itself is contracting, on a schedule set by climate rather than by any commodity cycle.

咖啡经历过糟糕的价格年份，并且都恢复了。这次不同之处在于：种植面积本身正在收缩，而这份时间表由气候决定，不由任何大宗商品周期决定。

4.4

Why This Is Not a Cyclical Bet为什么这不是一轮周期性押注

Coffee demand is inelastic and consumption is habitual; the plant is a 30-year asset; the shock is climatically forced. Seedling demand is therefore driven by biology and weather, not by GDP or by consumer sentiment cycles.

咖啡需求刚性、消费习惯化；植株是 30 年资产；冲击来自气候。因此幼苗需求由生物学与天气驱动，而非 GDP 或消费信心周期。

The three-part structural argument 三段式结构性论证

1 · The replacement clock1 · 替换时钟

Coffee bushes are replaced on a biological schedule that cannot be deferred indefinitely. Unlike discretionary consumer categories, this spending happens in bad years and good ones.

咖啡树按生物周期被替换，无法无限期推迟。与可选消费品不同，这项支出在景气与不景气年份都要发生。

2 · The climate accelerant2 · 气候加速器

Every El Niño cycle front-loads replanting decisions that would otherwise have taken a decade. Shock events compress demand into short windows.

每一轮厄尔尼诺都会把原本要花十年做出的复种决策提前。冲击事件把需求压缩进短窗口。

3 · The localisation pull3 · 本地化拉动

Imported seedlings carry quarantine cost, freight and 3–6 month lead times. A production system that works anywhere with power and water removes the geography constraint entirely.

进口苗木承担检疫成本、运费与 3–6 个月交期。一套只要有电和水就能运转的生产系统，直接消除了地理约束。
