7.3

Does aeroponics hit the pain? A four-by-four mapping二 · 雾化水培模式是否命中痛点：四对四映射

Point by point. Your pain on the left; what we commit to deliver on the right — and how each claim gets tested.

逐条对照。左边是你们的痛点，右边是我们要交付的东西 —— 以及衡量它是否成立的方法。

Dimension维度 BeanStar's pain比星的痛点 The aeroponic answer雾化水培的解法 Verifiable metrics — we do not accept verbal promises可验证指标（我们不接受口头承诺）
Predictable可预测 Weather-dependent. Shortage and disruption risk; buyers worry daily; purchase prices jump with origin climate and FX — no annual budget is possible.靠天吃饭。面临减产、断供风险，加盟商天天为豆子发愁；采购价随产区气候与汇率跳动，无法做年度预算。 Gas-liquid aeroponic full-cycle management, unconstrained by climate, season or geography. 365 days a year, output is stable and schedulable.气液式雾化全周期管理，不受气候、季节、地理限制。全年 365 天，每天产出稳定、可排产。 ① Weekly output deviation over 12 consecutive months (target < ±5%); ② annual unit-cost volatility vs the ICE futures in the same window; ③ on-time delivery ≥ 98%.① 连续 12 个月的周度产量偏差（目标 < ±5%）；② 全年单位成本波动率与同期 ICE 期货波动率对比；③ 交付准时率（OTD）≥ 98%。
Replicable可复制 Traditional specialty varies by batch; blending is hard. What a customer drinks today may differ tomorrow — and 'a cup that is just right, every day' cannot have surprises.传统精品豆批次差异大，拼配难度高。消费者今天喝的和明天喝的味道可能不同 —— 而「一杯刚刚好的日常所需」不能有意外。 Nutrient formulation and environment parameters are programmable, so flavour is 'programmable': set to a citrus profile today, and next month the cupping profile still reproduces at high similarity.通过编程营养液配方与环境参数，实现风味「可编程」：今天设定为柑橘调性，下个月依然能复刻出高度相似的杯测表现。 ① Blind-test consistency across batches (scored each batch, reports open); ② lab re-tests of key flavour compounds (e.g. organic-acid profile); ③ a third-party cupping run by your sensory team — we only supply samples.① 跨批次盲测一致性（每批次盲测打分，报告公开）；② 关键风味物质（如有机酸谱）实验室复测；③ 由贵方感官团队主导、我们只提供样品的第三方杯测。
Engineerable可工程化 Traditional specialty is expensive, and buying more makes it dearer; the import leg stacks FX, logistics and tariffs on top.传统精品豆成本高，规模化采购反而导致价格上涨；进口链路上还叠加汇率、物流与关税。 Turn farming into manufacturing. Mist delivery sharply raises water-and-fertiliser efficiency, so marginal cost falls with scale — the opposite of traditional agriculture; local production removes the cross-border leg entirely.把农业变成工业。雾化大幅提高水肥利用效率，规模化后边际成本递减（与传统农业的边际成本递增相反）；本地化生产省去跨境物流与关税环节。 ① A full-lifecycle cost (LCOC) curve per modular capacity step; ② measured water, power and nutrient units per kg of green bean; ③ the CAPEX and payback band from the current base to the target capacity.① 按模块化产能测算的全生命周期成本（LCOC）曲线；② 每公斤生豆的水、电、肥单位消耗实测值；③ 从现有基地到目标产能的资本开支与回收期区间。
Patent & ESG专利与 ESG A new brand story and a new capital narrative are needed; the 1,000-store target and the next round both require a differentiator that can be verified.需要新的品牌故事与资本叙事；千店目标与下一轮融资，都需要一个能被验证的差异化支点。 'Full-cycle cultivation management method and system based on gas-liquid mist' — patent through preliminary examination. Zero-carbon, pesticide-free, water-saving, local short chain.《基于气液式雾化的全周期种植管理方法及系统》专利已初审合格。零碳、无农药、节水、本地短链。 ① A licensing frame so the patent can power co-branded 'zero-carbon / tech-cultivation limited' labels in store; ② an auditable water / carbon / zero-pesticide inventory; ③ on-wall, on-menu technical endorsement material for stores.① 专利用于门店端联名与「零碳／科技种植限定」标签的授权框架；② 可审计的水耗／碳排／农药零使用清单；③ 门店端可上墙、可写进菜单的技术背书素材。

Honest boundaries — what we cannot do yet, stated first诚实边界 · 我们必须先说清楚我们现在做不到什么

A document that only lists advantages has no value. Four boundary conditions, disclosed up front:一份只讲优点的文件没有价值。以下四条是我们主动交底的边界条件：

  • Early CAPEX is high. We cannot beat traditional sourcing on cost today — our advantage appears on the upper half of the curve, after capacity ramps.前期资本开支高。我们无法在成本上立刻打败传统采购 —— 我们的优势发生在产能爬坡之后的曲线上半段。
  • We are still at the seedling stage. We cannot serve thousand-store volumes today; any expectation of 'immediate supply' is not valid.我们仍在幼苗期。当前不具备承接千店级体量的量产能力，任何「立刻供货」的期待目前都不成立。
  • The mass-market line is out of our reach. The cost floor of the ¥15 band is commercial beans; aeroponics cannot get there yet, and we will not pretend otherwise.大众线我们碰不了。15 元价格带的主力产品，成本极限是商业豆，雾化水培现阶段打不下来，我们也不打算假装可以。
  • Flavour reproduction must be measured, not promised. What we offer is a hypothesis your sensory team is free to falsify — not marketing copy.风味复刻的相似度需要实测，不接受口头承诺。我们给出的是可被你们的感官团队否定的假设，不是营销话术。
