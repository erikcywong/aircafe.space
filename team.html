Two Co-Founders With a Documented Operating Record

两位联合创始人，一条可查记录

We are not going to present a team slide that looks bigger than the company. Today AIRCAFE has two co-founders — one on hardware and systems, one on operations and commercial scale — plus a defined plan to add three people with this round's capital. What we can show instead is the part that is usually missing at this stage: a documented sequence of validated results — a working prototype, an independent cupping score, a deployed sensor network, and a filed invention patent — all produced by the people listed on this page.

我们不会做一张比公司实际情况更"好看"的团队页。今天AIRCAFE由两位联合创始人组成——一位负责硬件与系统，一位负责营运与商业规模化，另有本轮资金到位后补入三个岗位的明确计划。我们能拿出来替代的部分，恰恰是这个阶段通常缺失的东西：一条可查的验证结果记录——可运行的原型机、独立杯测分数、已部署的传感器网络、已提交的发明专利，全部出自本页所列的人。

Team Risk, Stated Plainly团队风险，直说

Risk风险 Why it is real为什么这是真风险 What this round does about it本轮资金做什么
Inventor dependency on the hardware side硬件条线对发明人的依赖 The atomisation and control know-how currently sits largely with James Zhuang. Two founders remove the classical single-point failure, but the technical knowledge is still concentrated in one of them.雾化与控制方面的工艺知识目前主要集中在 James Zhuang 身上。两位创始人消除了经典的「单点故障」，但技术知识仍集中在其中一人身上。 Milestone #1 is parameter-library documentation; 36-month share lock-up for both co-founders; knowledge transfer is a funded deliverable, not a good intention.里程碑 #1 即参数库文档化；两位联合创始人股权均锁定 36 个月；知识转移是被预算的交付物，而不是一个善意的想法。
No agronomy PhD on staff yet目前尚无农学博士在职 The founding pair covers hardware and operations, not plant science. They can produce a validated pilot and cannot, between them, defend a cultivation method under substantive examination.开发者出身的创始人可以做出已验证的中试，但无法独自在实质审查中为一项栽培方法辩护。 The Head of Agronomy seat sits inside the funded people-and-compliance allocation, not as a future aspiration.农学负责人岗位明确列入已预算的人才与合规科目，不是未来的愿望。
Hiring risk in a niche discipline细分领域的招聘风险 Controlled-environment agriculture in China is a thin talent pool and competes with much better-funded vertical-farm companies.中国的设施环境农业人才池很薄，且要与资金充裕得多的垂直农场公司抢人。 Two of three seats are scoped as senior-but-not-executive, and the round is priced so that equity can be used as the recruiting instrument.三个岗位中有两个定位为高级但非高管岗，且本轮定价预留了以股权作为招聘工具的空间。
Two founders are still a thin team两位创始人仍然是薄团队 Two people cannot simultaneously run investor process, patent prosecution, hiring and the first commercial customer without one of them slipping.两个人无法同时推进融资流程、专利审查、招聘与首个商业客户而不让其中一项滑落。 Tranche release is tied to milestones rather than to activity, so a slipped function is visible at the next release point rather than at the end.资金按里程碑而非按活动释放，因此某一职能滑落会在下一个释放点被看见，而不是等到最后。
