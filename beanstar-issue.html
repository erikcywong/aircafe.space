7.2 · Supply Diagnosis7.2 · 供应链诊断 The deadlock死结诊断 BeanStar · 比星咖啡 2026-10-09

Specialty scarcity versus franchise scale — a deadlock with three exits, all of them bad.

「精品低价 + 加盟扩张」的供应链死结 —— 三个出口，个个有代价。

Specialty beans are scarce and non-replicable; franchise expansion needs standardisation at compressed cost. We show why the contradiction is mathematical, walk the three collapse paths, and put the 2024–2026 price record on the table as evidence.

精品豆稀缺而不可复制，加盟扩张需要极致压缩成本下的标准化。我们说明这个矛盾为什么是数学性的，走完三条塌陷路径，并把 2024–2026 的价格记录摆上桌作为证据。

7.2

The deadlock: specialty pricing at franchise scale一 · 「精品低价 + 加盟扩张」的供应链死结

State the contradiction first. It is not an operating problem — it is two physical realities colliding.

先把矛盾说清楚。它不是经营问题，而是两条物理规律撞在一起。

Specialty beans are, by nature, scarce and non-replicable精品豆的本质，是稀缺性与风土不可复制

Traditional specialty beans depend on specific micro-climates, altitude and soil (terroir). Effort cannot overcome this; it is a boundary set by nature:传统精品豆依赖特定微气候、海拔、土壤（风土 / terroir）。这不是努力可以克服的，是自然条件给定的边界：

  • A yield ceiling — a given estate's Geisha produces what it produces; it does not double because BeanStar adds 700 stores.产量有天花板 —— 某个庄园的瑰夏，一年产量就那么多，不会因为比星多开 700 家店就凭空倍增。
  • Violent price swings — climate disasters (Brazil frost, Ethiopia drought) cut harvests and spike auction prices. In Feb 2025 arabica futures hit $4.41/lb, more than double a year earlier.价格波动剧烈 —— 气候灾害（巴西霜冻、埃塞干旱）直接减产，拍卖价随之飙升。2025 年 2 月阿拉比卡期货冲到 $4.41/磅的历史最高点，是 2024 年同期的两倍以上。
  • Batch variation — even within one estate, flavour differs across years and lots. For 'a cup that is just right, every day', that is fatal.批次差异大 —— 即使是同一庄园，不同年份、不同批次，风味也会有差异。这对「一杯刚刚好的日常所需」是致命的。

Franchise expansion is, by nature, standardisation at compressed cost加盟扩张的本质，是标准化与成本极致压缩

  • Franchisees need a model they can calculate: inputs that never rise in price, never run short, and never vary in quality.加盟商需要能算得清的利润模型：原材料常年不涨价、不断供、品质绝对一致。
  • 'Low price' means BeanStar cannot pass specialty-bean costs through to consumers without limit, the way Manner or Seesaw can — the ¥15–20 band is hard.「低价」意味着比星不能像 Manner 或 Seesaw 那样，把精品豆的成本无上限地转嫁给消费者 —— 15–20 元的价格带是硬的。
  • So the supplier is squeezed in the middle: absorbing spot-market volatility it cannot pass on to the store-level P&L.于是供应商被夹在中间：既要承担精品豆的采买波动，又不能把波动传导到加盟商的门店模型里。

Where it breaks: the bigger the scale, the tighter the triangle矛盾爆发点：规模越大，三角越紧

As BeanStar moves from ~300 operating stores toward 1,000 and beyond, there are only three ways to walk — each with a price:当比星从 300 家在营门店走向 1,000 家甚至更大规模时，只有三种走法，每一种都有代价：

Path A · keep truly traditional specialty beans路径 A · 坚持真正的传统精品豆

Volume cannot keep up, and prices will surge. Every step up in purchase volume means paying more, to scarcer, more distant origins.量跟不上，价格会暴涨。采购量每上一个台阶，都意味着向更稀缺、更贵、更远的产区加价拿货。

Result: the 'low price' promise breaks, and franchisee margins collapse.结果：打破「低价」承诺，加盟商利润崩盘。

Path B · switch to commercial beans to hold volume and price路径 B · 改用商业豆保量降价

The 'specialty' position collapses. SCA 85+ sourcing and the ICT platinum-medal backing could no longer be reproduced in the cup.「精品」定位坍塌。SCA 85+ 的选豆标准和 ICT 铂金奖的品质背书，将无法在杯子里被复现。

Result: the brand loses its pricing power, and customers notice.结果：品牌失去溢价能力，消费者不买账。

Path C · depend entirely on imports路径 C · 完全依赖进口

FX, cross-border logistics and geopolitics can choke the line at any time. In a highly concentrated origin market, one tariff, one shipping disruption, one drought re-prices the whole chain.汇率、跨境物流、地缘政治随时卡脖子。产区集中度极高的市场里，一次关税、一次航道中断、一次干旱，就足以重定价整条链。

Result: supply certainty is hostage to external variables.结果：供应确定性受制于外部变量。

BeanStar's supply chain today is a tightrope strung between 'specialty' and 'low price'. The biggest pain ahead is not a shortage of beans — it is the absence of a predictable, replicable, engineering-grade base of stable specialty supply.比星现在的供应链，其实是悬在「精品」和「低价」之间的一根钢丝。未来最大的痛点，不是没有豆子，而是没有「可预测、可复制、可工程化」的稳定精品豆供应底座。

Conclusion of Part One结论

The evidence: this has already happened, not hypothesised证据：这不是理论推演，是已经发生过的事

2024–2026 gave the global coffee chain a full demonstration of how external variables break a cost model:2024–2026 年，全球咖啡产业链完整演示了一遍「外部变量如何击穿成本模型」：

Why the price moved like that价格为什么会这样动

  • Brazil — about one third of world output — saw consecutive droughts; the key state of Minas Gerais had almost no rain through its flowering window in 2024.巴西（全球约 1/3 产量）2024 年遭遇连续干旱，主产州米纳斯吉拉斯雨季几乎无雨。
  • Vietnam, the largest robusta origin, lost about 10% for several consecutive years, pushing buyers into arabica and adding demand.越南（最大罗布斯塔产地）连续多年减产约 10%，买家转向阿拉比卡，进一步推高需求。
  • Certified exchange stocks fell to about 13,000 t — a 27-year low; the buffer effectively vanished.交易所认证库存降至约 1.3 万吨 —— 27 年最低，缓冲垫几乎消失。
  • Tariffs and geopolitics re-routed trade flows; importers rebuilt supply paths at higher cost and complexity.关税与地缘政治扰动贸易流向，进口商被迫重构供应路径，成本与复杂度同步上升。
  • Speculative money amplified a fundamental shortage into a price spike.投机资金入场，把基本面短缺放大成价格尖峰。

What it means for a coffee chain对连锁咖啡意味着什么

  • Coffee is typically 25–35% of a café's COGS. A 30–40% green-bean spike breaks the single-store model outright.咖啡通常占咖啡馆 COGS 的 25–35%。生豆涨 30–40%，单店模型会被直接击穿。
  • Retail lags futures: futures peaked in Feb 2025; retail prices followed 14 months later.零售端的反应永远滞后：期货 2025 年 2 月见顶，零售价格 14 个月后才开始跟进。
  • Origin concentration (Brazil + Vietnam) means no substitute can step in quickly.产区高度集中（巴西 + 越南），意味着没有替代方案可以快速补位。
  • Whoever turns green-bean cost into a budgetable fixed item gains pricing initiative nobody else has.结论：谁把生豆成本变成可预算的固定项，谁就获得了别人没有的定价主动权。

One specific fact that must be put on the table一个必须点出的具体事实

By public reporting, in Mar 2026 BeanStar opened its first pour-over experience store in Shenzhen with the ¥18 'pour-over freedom pass', and in Apr 2026 launched 'free upgrade to Geisha, all year'.据公开报道，比星在 2026 年 3 月于深圳开出首家手冲体验店并推出 18 元「手冲自由卡」，4 月进一步推出「全年免费升瑰夏」策略。

As brand-building and customer acquisition, this is a beautiful move. But seen from the supply side it pushes the triangle to its limit: the lowest price promise, bound to the most expensive and most volatile bean category there is. Geisha is among the most supply-limited and price-elastic specialty varieties in the world. Every additional quarter this promise runs, and every store it covers, raises the demand on upstream stability exponentially.从品牌与获客角度看，这是很漂亮的一手。但从供应链角度看，它把三角张力推到了极限 —— 用最低的售价承诺，绑定成本最高、波动最剧烈的豆种。瑰夏是全世界产量最受限、价格弹性最大的精品品种之一。这项承诺每延长一个季度、每多覆盖一家门店，对上游稳定供应能力的要求就指数级上升。

This is not criticism. Quite the opposite: precisely because BeanStar dares to make this promise at the brand level, it needs a supply base that can turn 'Geisha-grade flavour' from an estate scarcity into a schedulable industrial product. That is exactly what we are building.这段没有批评的意思。恰恰相反：正因为贵方敢在品牌端做这种承诺，才更需要一个能把「瑰夏级风味」从「庄园稀缺品」变成「可排产的工业品」的供应底座。这正是我们在做的事。
