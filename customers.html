Who Actually Writes the Cheque

真正开支票的是谁

Our customer is not the consumer holding a cup. It is the plantation with a worn-out block, the nursery that already owns the channel, and the agency with a replanting budget. This page is who they are, who else is selling to them, and where the first modules physically land.

我们的客户不是端着杯子的消费者，而是地块老化的种植园、已拥有渠道的苗圃、以及握有复种预算的机构。本页讲的是他们是谁、还有谁在卖给他们，以及第一批模块物理上落在哪里。

4.3

Who Actually Pays谁真正付钱

Our customer is not the consumer holding a cup. It is four types of buyer, each with a capital-sized, repeating reason to purchase tens of thousands of plants at a time.

我们的客户不是端着杯子的消费者，而是四类买方——每一类都有资本开支级别的、可重复发生的理由，一次性采购数万株。

Coffee plantations & estates咖啡种植园与庄园

A 30-year block reaching end-of-life, or a block hit by drought or rust, is a scheduled replant. The decision is capex-sized and recurring — they buy in tens of thousands of plants, not units.

30 年生的地块进入生命周期末端，或遭旱灾、叶锈病打击，就是一次排定复种。这是一项资本开支级别的、可重复发生的决策——他们按数万株采购，而不是按株。

Nurseries & seedling wholesalers苗圃与种苗批发商

Our most natural first customer. They already own the distribution, and their binding constraint is cycle time and survival rate — exactly the two metrics our process moves most.

最自然的第一批客户。他们已拥有渠道，其核心制约正是周期与成活率——也正是我们的工艺改善幅度最大的两项指标。

Replanting & industry programmes复种计划与产业项目

Producing-country governments and industry bodies fund replanting and varietal-renewal programmes. These are tender-based, localisation-sympathetic and explicitly interested in water and pesticide reduction.

产区政府与行业机构资助复种与品种更新计划。这类客户以招标采购，偏好本地化，且明确关注节水与减药指标。

Specialty & varietal projects精品与特色品种项目

Buyers who need provenance and consistency: a roaster building a single-origin supply story, a coffee-chain OEM programme, or a project propagating heat-tolerant varieties. Smaller volume, materially higher price.

需要溯源与一致性的买方：构建单一产地供应叙事的烘焙商、咖啡连锁的 OEM 计划，或扩繁耐热品种的项目。量小，但单价显著更高。

Why the first two matter most. Estates and nurseries both buy on cycle time and survival, both already hold distribution, and neither needs us to build a consumer brand first. That is why the first commercial sale is targeted there, and not at a roaster or a retail chain — where the sales cycle is longer and the specification is stricter.

为什么前两类最重要。种植园与苗圃都按周期与成活率采购，都已握有渠道，也都不需要我们先把消费品牌建起来。这就是为什么首批商业销售锚定在这两类，而不是烘焙商或零售连锁——后者的销售周期更长，规格要求也更严。

Lead times, unit prices and contract sizes by customer type are not stated here because we have no executed contracts to base them on. That evidence is the M3 milestone.此处不列各类客户的交期、单价与合同规模，因为我们还没有已签署合同可以作为依据。那份证据正是 M3 里程碑要交付的内容。

4.4

Why This Is Not a Cyclical Bet为什么这不是一轮周期性押注

Coffee demand is inelastic and consumption is habitual; the plant is a 30-year asset; the shock is climatically forced. Seedling demand is therefore driven by biology and weather, not by GDP or by consumer sentiment cycles.

咖啡需求刚性、消费习惯化；植株是 30 年资产；冲击来自气候。因此幼苗需求由生物学与天气驱动，而非 GDP 或消费信心周期。

The three-part structural argument 三段式结构性论证

1 · The replacement clock1 · 替换时钟

Coffee bushes are replaced on a biological schedule that cannot be deferred indefinitely. Unlike discretionary consumer categories, this spending happens in bad years and good ones.

咖啡树按生物周期被替换，无法无限期推迟。与可选消费品不同，这项支出在景气与不景气年份都要发生。

2 · The climate accelerant2 · 气候加速器

Every El Niño cycle front-loads replanting decisions that would otherwise have taken a decade. Shock events compress demand into short windows.

每一轮厄尔尼诺都会把原本要花十年做出的复种决策提前。冲击事件把需求压缩进短窗口。

3 · The localisation pull3 · 本地化拉动

Imported seedlings carry quarantine cost, freight and 3–6 month lead times. A production system that works anywhere with power and water removes the geography constraint entirely.

进口苗木承担检疫成本、运费与 3–6 个月交期。一套只要有电和水就能运转的生产系统，直接消除了地理约束。

Where the Modules Go模块部署到哪里

Deployment follows the demand, not the map. The sequence below is ordered by how quickly a paying customer can be reached, not by how impressive the geography looks.

部署跟随需求，而不是地图。下面的顺序按"多快能接触到付费客户"排列，而不是按地理版图有多好看排列。

China · Yunnan & Hainan中国 · 云南与海南Reference market: domestic coffee belt, replanting programmes, close to base. Module #1 belongs here.基准市场：国内咖啡带、复种计划、贴近基地。模块 #1 应该放在这里。
→
Southeast Asia · Vietnam & Indonesia东南亚 · 越南与印尼Robusta-to-arabica transition pressure; existing portfolio distribution; heat and rust exposure make indoor production attractive罗布斯塔转阿拉比卡的压力；已有被投渠道；高温与叶锈暴露让室内生产更具吸引力
→
Middle East · Gulf中东 · 海湾地区Zero domestic arabica today, high willingness to pay, sovereign food-security budgets — the highest-margin deployment目前本土无阿拉比卡种植、支付意愿高、有主权粮食安全预算——毛利最高的部署地
→
Latin America · Brazil & Colombia拉美 · 巴西与哥伦比亚Largest replanting volumes and strongest nursery channel, but also the lowest accepted price — enters last复种量最大、苗圃渠道最强，但可接受价格也最低——最后进入

This ordering is our own judgement about where a first sale is most achievable. It is not a market-entry plan with committed partners — no distributor agreement or letter of intent exists in any of these markets today.这个顺序是我们对"哪里最可能实现首单"的判断，不是一份有已承诺合作方的市场进入计划——今天在上述任何一个市场都不存在分销协议或意向书。
