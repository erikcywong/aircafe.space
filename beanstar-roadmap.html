1 · This stage does not chase a signature本阶段不追求签约

The goal is to turn 'can aeroponic beans reliably reproduce specialty flavour?' from a matter of position into a technical question that data can answer.目标是让「雾化水培的豆子能不能稳定复现精品风味」这个问题，从立场之争变成一个可以用数据回答的技术问题。

2 · All conclusions under your system所有结论以贵方体系为准

We do not issue our own quality verdicts. Cupping, sensory and cost definitions all follow BeanStar's existing standards; we only supply inputs.我们自己不出具品质结论。杯测、感官、成本口径，全部以比星既有的评估标准为准，我们只提供输入。

3 · A failed validation is still a valid output验证失败也是有效产出

If flavour or cost is falsified on any dimension, we will say so plainly and adjust the technical route — that is worth more than a vague letter of intent.若风味或成本任一维度被证伪，我们会如实告知并调整技术路线 —— 这比一份含糊的意向书更有价值。
