7.1 · Supply Diagnosis7.1 · 供应链诊断 One page摘要 BeanStar · 比星咖啡 2026-10-09

You are looking for a way out between “specialty” and “low price” — and the key we hold happens to be engineered.

贵方正在为「精品」与「低价」之间找一条出路 —— 而我们手上那把钥匙，恰好是工程化的。

This document is not a sales pitch; it is a structural diagnosis. We take BeanStar's model down to its foundations, expose a tension that has nothing to do with execution or effort, then explain why gas-liquid aeroponic cultivation sits exactly on its solution — and are equally explicit about what we cannot do yet.

这份文件不是销售提案，而是一次结构性的诊断。我们把比星咖啡当下的商业模式拆到最底层，指出一个与执行力无关、与努力程度无关的结构性张力，然后说明我们（AIRCAFE · 气液式雾化咖啡种植）为什么恰好落在它的解法上 —— 也同样说明我们现在还不能做什么。

7.1

One page: your position, the real pain, our place一页看懂：贵方的处境、真正的痛点、我们的位置

If you only read for three minutes, read this page.

如果只读三分钟，读这一页。

① Where you stand today① 你们现在在哪

First store in Suzhou, June 2022. Four rounds closed in three years — Shunwei Capital (angel), Zhengxuan Capital (Series A), and a Series B led by the Suzhou Nongfa fund in Nov 2025 — nearly ¥100m in total.2022 年 6 月苏州首店，三年跑完四轮融资 —— 顺为资本天使轮、正煊资本 A 轮、2025 年 11 月苏州农发产业科创基金领投的 B 轮，累计近亿元。

By end-2025: 700+ signed stores, nearly 300 operating, across 50+ cities at home and abroad. The 2026 target is 1,000 stores.2025 年底签约门店 700 家以上、在营近 300 家，覆盖海内外 50 多个城市；2026 年的目标是冲刺千店。

Positioned at the ¥15–20 'affordable specialty' band, differentiated by signature creative drinks; store gross margin around 70%, most stores profitable.定位 15–20 元「平价精品」，用创意特调做差异化，单店毛利约 70%，多数门店盈利。

② Where the real pain sits② 真正的痛点在哪

Not 'running out of beans' — but not having a predictable, replicable, engineering-grade base of stable specialty supply.不是「没有豆子」。是没有一条可预测、可复制、可工程化的稳定精品豆供应底座。

The gap widens toward 1,000 stores: the specialty yield ceiling, violent price swings and batch variation all collide with franchise standardisation at the same time.越往千店走，这个缺口越大：传统精品豆的产量天花板、价格剧烈波动、批次差异，三件事同时与「加盟标准化」对冲。

The 2026 'free Geisha upgrade, all year' and the ¥18 pour-over pass bind the lowest price promise to the most expensive, most volatile bean category — pushing the tension to its limit.2026 年推出的「全年免费升瑰夏」与「18 元手冲自由卡」，等于用最低的售价承诺绑定了成本最高、波动最大的豆种 —— 把张力推到了极限。

③ Where we come in③ 我们的位置

Gas-liquid aeroponic full-cycle cultivation — no terroir dependency, no climate or season constraint, flavour parameters that are programmable.气液式雾化（Aeroponic）全周期种植管理 —— 不依赖风土、不受气候与季节约束、风味参数可编程。

The role we claim is not supplier, but 'technology insurance and joint-validation partner for BeanStar's specialty supply chain'.我们主张的定位不是供应商，而是「比星精品供应链的科技保险与联合验证伙伴」。

At this stage we ask for one small thing: use your stores as the first industrial validation scene for this technology.现阶段只求一件小事：把你们的门店，作为这项技术的首个产业验证场景。

At the current stage, our position is: the technology-insurance and joint-validation partner of BeanStar's specialty supply chain.比星正在为「精品」与「低价」之间的矛盾寻找出路，而我们的雾化水培专利技术，恰好是那把「解开死结的工程化钥匙」。当前阶段，我们的定位是 —— 比星精品供应链的科技保险与联合验证伙伴。
