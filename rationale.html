5.1 · Why a Trial Field, Specifically5.1 · 为什么需要一块试验田 5.1b · US$300,000 Trial Field5.1b · 30 万美金试验田

US$300,000 to Prove the Whole Cycle

用 30 万美金，验证整个周期

The usual version of this argument asks an investor to believe in the thesis and then find a customer later. Ours is the reverse: your coffee portfolio already contains a verified demand signal, and US$300,000 buys a working plant on that demand curve — carried all the way from seedling to fruit.

这类论证的常规写法，是请投资人先相信逻辑、之后再找客户。我们是反过来的：你们的咖啡投资组合里已经有一个经过验证的需求信号，而 30 万美金是在那条需求曲线上买下一株真正跑完全程的植株——从幼苗一直带到挂果。

5.1

Why a Trial Field, Specifically为什么需要一块试验田

1 · You invest early, and we are early1 · 你们投早，而我们正好早

AIRCAFE's publicly stated posture is "invest early, invest small," with A and B rounds as the core. This is the window where institutional capital normally does not engage and where your stated mandate does. Entry valuation today and entry valuation in twelve months are not the same number.

AIRCAFE公开的投资主张是「投早、投小」，以 A/B 轮为主。现在正是机构资本通常不进场、而你们明确会进场的窗口。今天的进入估值与十二个月后的进入估值不是同一个数字。

2 · Dual-carbon technology is on your list2 · 双碳科技在你们的清单上

AIRCAFE lists dual-carbon technology among its focus areas. Every seedling we produce is a quantifiable unit of water saved, pesticide avoided and carbon not emitted — with a per-batch, per-module audit trail rather than a headline claim.

双碳科技是AIRCAFE列明的关注领域。我们产出的每一株苗都是一个可计量的节水、减药与减排单元，附有按批次、按模块的审计链条，而非一句宣传。

3 · This is an AI + hardware business in disguise3 · 这本质上是一门 AI + 硬件的生意

Strip away the leaves and the aeroponic system is a precision environment-control machine: sensors, atomisation actuators, controllers, and a scheduling algorithm. That is the exact capability structure AIRCAFE built its track record on.

剥掉叶子，雾培系统本质是一台精密环境控制设备：传感器、雾化执行器、控制器与调度算法。这正是AIRCAFE建立业绩记录的那套能力结构。

4 · Industrial internet, applied to agriculture4 · 产业互联网，落在农业上

Our output is not only seedlings — it is a standardised production unit plus a parameter pack. Agriculture moving from craft to process is the same transition AIRCAFE has repeatedly backed in manufacturing.

我们交付的不只是苗，而是一个标准化生产单元加一套参数包。农业从手艺走向工艺，与AIRCAFE在制造业反复下注的那次转变是同一次。

5 · The Xiaomi supply chain is a real multiplier5 · 小米供应链是真正的放大器

Atomisation nozzles, sensors, LED arrays and controllers all ride consumer-electronics cost and yield curves. AIRCAFE can bring a supply-chain capability that a purely financial investor cannot — and it directly attacks our largest cost line.

雾化喷嘴、传感器、LED 阵列与控制器都搭在消费电子的成本与良率曲线上。AIRCAFE能带来纯财务投资人给不了的供应链能力——而这正对着我们最大的成本项。

6 · Overlapping geographies6 · 地理版图重合

Vietnam, Indonesia and Brazil are both coffee belts and markets where AIRCAFE's portfolio companies already operate. Module deployment and portfolio distribution are not separate problems.

越南、印尼、巴西既是咖啡带，也是AIRCAFE被投企业已在经营的市场。模块部署与被投渠道并非两个独立问题。

5.1b

The Trial Field: US$300,000 to Take the Plant From Seedling to Fruit试验田：用 30 万美金，把这株植物从幼苗带到挂果

US$300,000 — about ¥2.13m at US$1 ≈ ¥7.1 — is a small cheque for a fund and a decisive one for us. Its purpose is narrow and deliberately unglamorous: to prove that the aeroponic process holds from the day a seedling goes in to the day a cherry comes off.

30 万美金（按 US$1 ≈ ¥7.1 折算约 ¥213 万）对一支基金而言是小额支票，对我们却是决定性的。它的用途窄得刻意、也刻意不性感：证明这套雾化种植工艺，从苗入舱那一天起、到果实采收那一天止，全程成立。

Why the whole cycle, and not just the nursery stage为什么必须是全周期，而不是只做育苗段

A coffee plant does not reveal what it is worth until it fruits. Nursery-stage proofs — germination rate, root density, survival — are necessary and cheap to obtain, and every hydroponic and tissue-culture competitor can show them. They are not the question a buyer of specialty green beans is actually asking.

一株咖啡在挂果之前，不会暴露它真正值多少。育苗段的证明——发芽率、根密度、成活率——是必要的、也容易拿到，每一个水培和组织培养的竞争者都能展示。但它们不是精品生豆买家真正要问的问题。

The question is whether the cup, the yield per plant and the cost per viable plant still hold once the plant has been carried through flowering, cherry set and ripening under an atomised root zone. That is a twelve-to-eighteen month observation with no shortcut, and it is exactly the window this tranche funds.

真正的问题是：当植株在雾化根区环境下走完开花、坐果、成熟之后，杯测风味、单株产量与单株可用苗成本是否依然成立。这是一段 12 到 18 个月、没有捷径的观察期，也正是这笔资金要买的窗口。

A number small enough to be said out loud一个小到可以当众说出口的数字

US$300,000 does not build the commercial module — at ≈¥3m capex, that is a later, larger decision. It buys the evidence that makes that later decision cheap to take. We chose this size deliberately: it is below the threshold at which a fund needs a new investment committee thesis, and above the threshold at which a trial can be run honestly.

30 万美金建不起商业模块——那需要约 ¥300 万资本开支，是更晚、更大的一次决定。它买到的是让那个更晚的决定变得「便宜可做」的证据。这个额度是我们刻意选的：它低于一支基金需要专门新开一个投委会议题的门槛，也高于「一次诚实的试验」所需的最低门槛。

If the trial field works, the follow-on conversation is about capacity and supply. If it does not, the loss is one small cheque and one honest memo — and you have learned something about the segment that no amount of desk research would have given you.

如果试验田成了，接下来的对话是关于产能与供应。如果没成，损失是一张小额支票和一份诚实的备忘录——而你们对这个赛段的认知，是任何案头研究都给不了的。

You are not being asked to believe in a hypothetical customer我们不是请你们去相信一个假想的客户

AIRCAFE led a specialty-coffee chain's angel round in 2022 to the tune of several tens of millions of yuan, followed on in its Series A in 2024, and the company has since closed a further round led by agriculture-industry funds. Few seed-stage agricultural investments arrive with a reference customer already sitting inside the same portfolio.

AIRCAFE 2022 年领投该连锁品牌数千万元天使轮，2024 年继续参与其 A 轮，该连锁其后又完成一轮由农业产业基金领投的融资。几乎没有一个农业种子期项目，能在同一投资组合内自带一个已存在的参考客户。

That is what changes this from a technology bet into a validation programme with a named counterparty.

这让它从一笔技术押注，变成一个有明确对手方的验证计划。

the chain's own success is what creates the opening该连锁自己的成功，恰恰打开了这个缺口

the chain sells specialty coffee at ¥15–20 a cup and scales through small franchise units. That model works beautifully at 300 stores and becomes arithmetic at 1,000 and 5,000: every new store is a permanent new claim on specialty-grade green beans, on identical flavour parameters, on a price the franchisee can still live with.

该连锁以 ¥15–20 的价格带卖精品咖啡，靠小型加盟店复制扩张。这套模型在 300 家店时非常漂亮，到了 1,000 家、5,000 家就变成一道算术题：每一家新店，都是对精品级生豆、对可复现的风味参数、对加盟商仍能承受的成本，新增一份永久索取权。

Franchise expansion is a demand engine. It is also, in this segment, a supply-chain liability — and the industry's own research says so: specialty demand is rising faster than specialty-grade supply, and the segment's real moat is supply-chain integration, cost control and consistent quality, not store count.

加盟扩张是一台需求发动机，但在精品这一段，它同时是一笔供应链负债——行业研究本身就是这么说的：精品需求增速快于精品级供给，这一赛段真正的护城河是供应链整合、成本控制与品质一致性，而不是门店数量。

What we are: an OEM upstream, defined by output我们是什么：一个由产出定义的 OEM 上游

We are not a plantation or a trading house with a story. We are a production system, and we would define our role by what comes out of it rather than by what we call ourselves.

我们不是带故事的种植园或贸易商。我们是一套生产系统，而且我们更愿意用它的产出来定义自己的角色，而不是用一个称号。

  • Certified seedling stock for own-brand or partner plantings — 92–96% survival, 4-month cycle, three batches a year.认证种苗，供自有品牌或合作方种植——成活率 92–96%、周期 4 个月、每年三批。
  • Reproducible flavour parameters — cup profile held within a coefficient of variation under 8% across batches, which is precisely what lets a chain promise the same cup in every city.可复现的风味参数——批次间杯测曲线变异系数低于 8%，这正是让一个连锁能在每座城市承诺同一杯味道的前提。
  • Cost structure nobody else can offer — ¥6.61 landed against a ¥25 selling price, on 90%+ less water and zero pesticide.别人给不出的成本结构——落地成本 ¥6.61 对应 ¥25 售价，用水减少 90%+、农药为零。
  • Geographic hedging — production moves indoors, so frost, drought and El Niño stop being supply events.地理风险对冲——生产移入室内，霜冻、干旱与厄尔尼诺不再是供应事件。

"High-value" here means the high end of the usable range, not the rarest lot in the world. the chain does not need a Cup of Excellence auction winner. It needs an eighty-plus cup with an industrial guarantee behind it — which is a far larger market, and one we can actually serve at volume.

这里的「高价值」指可用区间的高端，而不是世界上最稀有的批次。该连锁不需要一支竞标冠军豆，它需要一杯 80+ 分、背后有工业级保证的咖啡——那是一个大得多的市场，也是我们真正能规模服务的市场。

Where the fit actually is — and where it honestly is not契合点到底在哪——以及它诚实地不在哪

Overclaiming here would be the fastest way to lose your team's trust, so we will draw the line precisely.

在这件事上夸大，是最快失去你们团队信任的方式，所以我们把界线画准。

  • Fits: the chain's specialty-SKU and consistency bottleneck. Short-cycle, contractible, verifiable batch by batch.契合：该连锁的精品 SKU 与一致性瓶颈。周期短、可签约、可逐批验证。
  • Fits: raw-material cost contraction at the franchisee level, where a ¥15–20 retail price is most exposed.契合：加盟商层面的原材料成本收缩，这正是 ¥15–20 零售价最吃紧的地方。
  • Fits: an ESG and origin narrative that can be printed on a menu — water, pesticide and transport distance, each with an audit trail.契合：可印在菜单上的 ESG 与原产地叙事——用水、农药与运输距离，每一项都有审计链条。
  • Does not fit — today: the chain's blend base and its highest-volume commodity lines. We have no volume for those yet, and we will not pretend to.尚不契合——今天：该连锁的拼配基豆与出货量最大的普通产品线。对这两条我们目前没有量，我们不会假装有。

So the honest phrasing is not "we will supply the chain." It is: we are a candidate upstream that the chain can test with, at a size the chain can afford, on a timeline our own milestone ladder can actually hit.

所以诚实的表述不是「我们将供应该连锁」，而是：我们是一个该连锁可以拿来做测试的上游候选，规模是该连锁负担得起的，时间表是我们自己的里程碑阶梯真能兑现的。

The trial field, stated plainly. US$300,000 buys one instrumented bay and the agronomy to run a coffee plant from seedling through flowering to fruit, plus the third-party verification that turns the result into evidence rather than a claim. It is deliberately sized below an investment-committee threshold and above the cost of an honest trial. If it works, the same money that bought a return also bought your portfolio a validated supply-chain position, decided before the chain has to commit to a supply contract, before we have to commit to capacity, and while the patent is still in examination. If it does not, you have bought information at seed-round pricing, and the bay, the parameter library and the patent family remain separable assets.

把「试验田」说清楚。30 万美金买下一间贯通全周期的试验舱、把一株咖啡从幼苗带到开花再到挂果所需的农学投入，以及把结果变成「证据」而非「说法」的第三方验证。这个额度刻意定在投资委员会门槛之下、诚实试验成本之上。如果它成了，同一笔钱既买到了回报，也为你们的组合买下了一个已验证的供应链位置——而这发生在该连锁不得不承诺供应合同之前、在我们不得不承诺产能之前、在专利仍在实审的时候。如果它没成，你们是以种子轮的定价买了一份信息，而试验舱、参数库与专利族仍作为可分离资产存在。
