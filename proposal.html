The Shape of the Deal, Before the Numbers

先谈交易结构，再谈数字

Nothing on this page is a binding offer, and no valuation figure is published here. What follows is the structure we would bring to a term sheet — so that the first conversation can be about the technology and the governance, not about reconstructing a price.

本页不构成任何有约束力的要约，也不公开任何估值数字。以下是我们在正式条款清单中会提出的结构——这样第一次对话可以谈技术与治理，而不必用来回推价格。

5.2

Indicative Transaction Framework示意性交易框架

Round Character轮次性质
Seed种子轮
Priced round with a staged release tied to the milestone ladder on the Traction pages.定价轮，资金按「幼苗期进展」各页所列的里程碑阶梯分批释放。
Ticket Structure出资结构
Lead + syndicate领投 + 跟投
Sized so that a single lead can carry the round, with room for a smaller co-investor. Amount and equity share are discussed bilaterally.规模设定为单一领投方可独立承担，同时保留一个小额跟投位。金额与股权比例双边商谈。

Deliberately not published. It is a function of the diligence outcome and of where the patent prosecution stands at signing.刻意不公开。它取决于尽调结果，以及签约时专利审查所处的阶段。
