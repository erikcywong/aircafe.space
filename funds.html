5.3 · Use of Funds5.3 · 资金用途 45% to the first commercial module45% 投入首个商业模块

Where the Seed Round Goes

本轮资金的去向

Four lines, and one of them takes almost half. If the first commercial module is not built, nothing else on this site matters — so the budget says so out loud.

四条线，其中一条占了近一半。如果首个商业模块建不起来，本网站其余内容都不重要——所以预算把这件事直接说了出来。

5.3

Use of Funds, by Share资金用途，按占比

Commercial validation module #1首个商业化验证模块45%

45% of the round — equipment and installation, site retrofit, and commissioning of one standard module at commercial geometry. This is the single item that converts a validated process into a sellable output.占本轮 45%——设备与安装、场地改造，以及一个商业几何尺寸标准模块的调试验收。这是把已验证工艺转化为可售产出的唯一关键项。

Process lock & patent family工艺固化与专利族扩展25%

25% of the round — recipe lock and multi-batch consistency verification; substantive examination support; extension filings covering nozzle configuration and control logic.占本轮 25%——配方锁定与多批次一致性验证；实质审查应对；就喷嘴结构与控制逻辑提交延伸申请。

Data, cupping & ESG baseline数据、杯测与 ESG 基线20%

20% of the round — independent cupping panel, accredited water-footprint and carbon-baseline accounting, and the certification pathway that later underpins premium pricing and green finance.占本轮 20%——独立杯测、经认可的用水与碳基线核算，以及后续支撑溢价定价与绿色融资的认证路径。

People, IP counsel & compliance人才、知识产权与合规10%

10% of the round — controlled-environment engineering and plant-nutrition hires, IP counsel, and statutory accounting capable of supporting a Series A process.占本轮 10%——设施环境工程与植物营养岗位、知识产权法律顾问，以及可支撑 A 轮流程的规范财务体系。
